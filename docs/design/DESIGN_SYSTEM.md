# Design System

> Replace token placeholders with values extracted from the approved Stitch project. Do not let agents invent a parallel visual language.

## Design Direction

AIVES should feel:
- professional,
- academic,
- calm,
- trustworthy,
- clear,
- accessible.

Avoid excessive:
- neon effects,
- decorative gradients,
- futuristic AI styling,
- motion that distracts during examination.

## Semantic AI Styling

AI-specific content may use a consistent semantic accent, but it must remain readable and professional.

Examples:
- AI Generated
- AI Suggested Score
- AI Follow-up
- AI Processing

AI styling must not imply final authority.

## Grading Hierarchy

### AI Suggested Score
Must clearly say it is suggested.

### Final Score
Must clearly indicate lecturer confirmation when confirmed.

The visual hierarchy must make official final data more authoritative than AI suggestions.

## Exam Environment

Active Viva UI prioritizes:
- question,
- speaking/listening state,
- answer timer,
- recording/transcript state,
- progress,
- connectivity.

Avoid unrelated navigation and decorative density.
