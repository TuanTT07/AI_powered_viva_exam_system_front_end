# Product — AIVES

## Name

**AI-powered Viva Exam System (AIVES)**

## Problem

Oral examinations are useful for project defenses, final exams and competency assessment, but they create operational problems:

- high lecturer time cost,
- inconsistent questions between exam rooms,
- inconsistent grading standards,
- poor scalability for large cohorts,
- limited objective evidence during grade disputes.

## Product Goal

AIVES supports the full oral-exam lifecycle:

1. prepare learning material,
2. build questions and rubrics,
3. create and schedule exams,
4. conduct adaptive AI-assisted Viva sessions,
5. produce AI-assisted grading evidence,
6. let lecturers confirm final grades,
7. preserve an auditable examination record,
8. provide reports and student feedback.

## Core Differentiator

AIVES is not a spoken multiple-choice system.

The AI behaves like a virtual examiner:

1. ask a main question,
2. receive the student's spoken answer,
3. convert speech to text,
4. analyze whether the answer is sufficient, unclear, incomplete or contradictory,
5. generate a targeted follow-up when appropriate,
6. continue until limits are reached or the response is sufficient.

## Core Product Principle

**Human in the loop.**

AI can:
- generate draft questions,
- generate follow-up questions,
- suggest rubric-based scores,
- suggest comments.

AI cannot:
- autonomously finalize the official grade.

The lecturer is the final grading authority.

## Product Roles

- Admin
- Lecturer
- Student

## Product Qualities

AIVES should feel:
- professional,
- academic,
- calm,
- trustworthy,
- transparent,
- accessible.

## Non-Functional Priorities

1. Low conversational latency
2. Reliable Vietnamese STT for academic terminology
3. Robust reconnect/error handling
4. Privacy and security of recordings/transcripts
5. Clear grading auditability
6. Strong role isolation
7. Human authority over final grading
